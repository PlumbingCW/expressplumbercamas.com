Sewer Camera Inspection in Camas, WA

A camera in the sewer line shows what a walkthrough cannot, and it is the cheapest way to avoid an expensive surprise. Call (360) 371-6092 to book an inspection before you buy a home or plan a dig.

What a Sewer Camera Inspection Shows

A waterproof camera on a flexible rod goes into the sewer line through a clean-out, and we watch the live image while recording. It shows pipe material, joints, roots, cracks, sags and anything that should not be there.

We note the distance to each finding and can locate it on the surface so the yard or driveway can be planned around it. You get the video and a plain description, not a pile of jargon.

When an Inspection Is Worth Booking

  • Before buying a home, especially an older one or one with mature trees near the line.
  • After repeated backups to find the cause.
  • Before a remodel that adds bathrooms or changes the drainage.
  • Before digging a new patio, driveway or addition over the line.
  • After cleaning to confirm the line is clear and sound.

If the camera finds a blockage, our sewer line cleaning service handles it. If it finds damage, the sewer line repair page explains the options.

Camera Findings and the Camas STEP System

Some Camas properties connect to the city's STEP system, where the owner is responsible for the line to the tank and the city owns the tank, pump and panel. A camera inspection shows the condition of the line you own, and a buyer should also ask the city about the tank serving the property.

Washington law requires a 811 call before digging, and utilities mark at least two business days ahead. If the video points to a dig, that lead time is built into the plan.

When a camera finds a defect, the repair is measured against the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) standards Washington adopts for slope, joints and materials.

How to Prepare for an Inspection

  • Make sure the cleanout is visible and reachable.
  • Tell the plumber about past backups and any old repairs.
  • Do not run water heavily right before the visit.
  • Note the age of the house and nearby trees.
  • Have the buyer or the agent available if the findings affect a purchase.

With that done, the visit is straightforward. You get the video, the distance to each finding and a plain explanation of what it means.

What Happens After the Camera Comes Out

We review the video with you and explain each finding in plain words. If the line is clear and sound, you have a record to keep. If there is a problem, we describe the repair options and what each would involve, so you can compare before you decide.

You are under no obligation to hire us for the repair. The inspection stands on its own, and the video is yours.

Buying or renovating near downtown Camas? A camera look at the line is one of the most useful checks to run before you commit.

Inspection Questions

How long does a sewer camera inspection take?

Most inspections take under an hour once we have access, and a blocked line adds time.

Do I get a copy of the video?

Yes. We record the inspection so you can keep it and share it with a buyer, a seller or a contractor.

Can an inspection find roots before they cause a backup?

Often yes, because roots show up at joints before they block the line.

Is an inspection useful for a newer home?

It can be. Builders sometimes leave debris in a new line, and a camera shows it.
