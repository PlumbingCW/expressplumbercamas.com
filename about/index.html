Who We Are

Camas Express Plumbing is a plumbing company serving Camas, Washington and nearby Clark County towns. We handle emergencies, repairs and planned installations for homes and small businesses, from drain cleaning to water heater replacement.

We are licensed and insured, and we keep our promises to what we can actually do. That means no invented credentials on this site and no claims we cannot back up.

How You Can Verify a Plumber in Washington

Washington requires plumbing contractors to register with the Department of Labor and Industries and to carry a bond and insurance. The L&I Verify a Contractor tool shows registration, workers' compensation status, bond lawsuits and violations, and we encourage you to use it on any contractor you hire.

What We Handle

Most of our calls fall into a handful of groups. Water heaters come first, from repairs to tankless installs. Drains and sewer lines come next, with hydro jetting and camera inspections for the stubborn ones.

Leaks and failed pipes round out the list, including leak detection and burst pipe repair. We also take on commercial plumbing for local shops and restaurants.

What We Do Not Do

We do not post prices on this site, because a plumbing price depends on what we find. We do not promise an arrival time, because traffic, weather and the job before yours are real. We do not use pressure tactics, and we do not start work until you agree to the price.

We also do not print a licence number, an insurer or a list of names on these pages. If you want to confirm who you are hiring, ask us on the phone and check the answer yourself on the state tool.

Why Camas Homes Need a Local Plumber

Camas has older downtown buildings, newer hillside subdivisions, STEP sewer connections and a water system with its own treatment steps. A plumber who knows those differences asks better questions at the door. The City of Camas Public Works Water Division serves about 23,500 consumers from groundwater wells, and it adds sodium hydroxide to reduce copper pipe corrosion, which matters when we look at a pinhole leak.

The city's building rules matter too. The Camas Building Division enforces the state-adopted Uniform Plumbing Code, with the 2021 codes in effect since March 15, 2024, so we ask what a project needs before we quote it.

What to Expect When You Call

A person answers and asks for your address, a callback number and what is happening. If water is running, the first thing we do is help you find the shutoff. Then we explain the likely cause, what the visit will involve and what we need from you.

When the plumber arrives, you hear the price and the plan before any tools come out. If something unexpected turns up behind a wall, we stop, explain it and ask before we continue. That is how we handle every job, large or small.

Our team keeps the work simple on purpose. We would rather spend ten minutes explaining a problem than send you a bill you do not understand, and we would rather tell you a repair is not worth it than sell you one that will not last.

If you have a question that is not about an emergency, call during the day and ask. We are happy to talk through a project, a quote from another plumber or a plumbing question that has been bothering you.

Everything on this site is written from what we do in the field. We do not copy competitors, and we do not use stock claims about awards or years in business. When something here changes, such as the codes we follow or the programs a utility offers, we update the page.
