Repeat Clog? Why Jetting Beats Another Cable

A cable cuts a channel through a blockage, but grease, scale and sludge stay on the pipe wall and rebuild. Hydro jetting uses pressurized water to scrub that layer off and flush it out.

The result is a line that drains at close to full diameter again. It is the right tool for heavy buildup, and the wrong tool for a pipe that has collapsed.

Camera first, jetter second

We look at the line before jetting an older pipe. If the pipe is broken or bellied, high pressure water can make the damage worse and a repair is the right call.

What Hydro Jetting Clears

  • Grease that has cooled and hardened in kitchen and restaurant lines.
  • Scale and mineral buildup that narrows older metal pipe.
  • Roots once they have been cut back, to scour the fine hairs left behind.
  • Sludge and soap packed into long horizontal runs.
  • Debris from wipes and paper that a cable could not fully remove.

Residential jobs usually start from a clean-out in the yard or basement. Commercial kitchens often have a dedicated access point, and our commercial plumbing page covers that side of the work.

Jetting Lines in Older Camas Buildings

Downtown Camas was rebuilt in brick after a 1923 fire destroyed the wooden storefronts, and many older buildings have drain lines that have seen decades of grease and scale. A camera look first tells us whether those lines can take high pressure water.

Homes on the city's STEP sewer system need extra care. The tank and pump belong to the city, so jetting stops at your own line, and if the outside alarm is sounding we recommend calling the city about the tank before running water.

No single code governs jetting, so we follow the pipe maker's guidelines and the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) rules for cleanouts, and a camera look first tells us what pressure the line can take.

What Happens During a Jetting Visit

We find the access point, usually a cleanout, and send a camera in first to look at the pipe. Then a hose with a special nozzle goes into the line. Water exits the nozzle in jets that scour the wall and push the debris along, and we retrieve it at the end of the run.

We finish with a second camera pass so you can see the result. If the pipe turns out to be in poor condition, we explain the repair options and why jetting alone was not enough.

Keeping a Jetted Line Clear

After jetting, keep grease and wipes out of the drain and use strainers at sinks. For businesses with heavy grease, a regular jetting schedule keeps buildup from returning. For homes, a camera look and a yearly check are usually enough.

If the line clogs again soon after jetting, that points to a structural problem like a belly or a crack, and a repair is the lasting fix.

Jetting Questions

Do you handle hydro jetting emergencies at night or on weekends in Camas?

Yes. When a restaurant drain or a main line backs up at night, the 24/7 line at (360) 371-6092 is open and we tell you what can be done that night.

Is hydro jetting safe for old pipes?

On sound pipe, yes. On cracked or collapsed pipe it can make things worse, which is why we scope the line first.

Will jetting remove tree roots?

It scours roots out of the pipe, but it does not stop them from regrowing if the pipe has a joint or crack. A repair is the lasting answer.

How often should a restaurant line be jetted?

It depends on the menu and the traps. A plan is set after seeing how fast buildup returns.
