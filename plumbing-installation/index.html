Plumbing Installation in Camas, WA

Remodels and additions go smoother when the plumbing is planned first. Call (360) 371-6092 to talk through new fixtures, new lines or a rough-in for a space you are building.

What We Install

Plumbing installation covers anything from a new bathroom sink to the lines for a whole addition. We run supply and drain lines, set fixtures and connect appliances, and we test everything before the walls close.

  • Fixtures such as toilets, sinks, tubs, showers and laundry hookups.
  • Supply lines in PEX or copper to new rooms.
  • Drain and vent lines sized for the load.
  • Gas piping for appliances where required.
  • Shutoff valves for every fixture.

Planning Plumbing for a Remodel or Addition

Plumbing is the most expensive thing to move once the walls are up. The best time to talk is when the layout is on paper, so drain slopes, vent paths and access panels are part of the design.

If the new space adds fixtures, check your water heater capacity as well, and our water heater installation page explains how we size it. For older homes, an inspection of existing supply lines may point to repiping during the same project.

Permits and Code for Camas Projects

The City of Camas Building Division administers the plumbing code, which in Washington is the Uniform Plumbing Code with state amendments under WAC 51-56. The 2021 I-Codes took effect in Camas on March 15, 2024, and new plumbing work usually needs a permit and inspection.

Washington requires plumbing contractors to be registered with the Department of Labor and Industries, and you can check any contractor on L&I's verification tool for registration, workers' comp status, bond claims and violations. Ask any plumber you are considering about it, including us.

Washington's rules for new plumbing start with the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), adopted with state amendments in WAC 51-56, and the contractor must be registered under RCW 18.106.

Common Installation Projects We See

  • Adding a bathroom or half bath in a basement or an attic.
  • Moving a kitchen sink or adding an island sink.
  • Installing a laundry hookup in a new location.
  • Running a new line to a detached garage or shop.
  • Replacing old fixtures during a remodel.

Each project begins with a visit to see the space, measure and talk through what you want. We explain what is possible, what will cost more and what can be done in stages, so you make decisions with the full picture.

Working With Your Contractor and Other Trades

Plumbing sits between framing, electrical and drywall, so timing matters. We coordinate with your builder, tell the other trades what we need and avoid holding up the project. If a design change affects the plumbing, we tell you early and explain what it means for the schedule and the price.

We also leave clear records of where lines run and where the shutoffs are, so you know what is behind the walls and can find it later.
