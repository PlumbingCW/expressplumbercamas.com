What a Water Heater Installation Involves

A new installation covers more than setting a tank on the floor. The supply and hot lines need connections, the venting or electrical circuit has to be right, and the relief valve must discharge to a safe place.

We walk through your utility room or garage first and note the clearances, the existing shutoff valves and any code items that have to change. That way the price you hear covers the whole job and not only the heater.

Choosing Where the Heater Goes

Placement affects everything from the length of the hot water pipe to how easily the unit can be serviced. Garages and utility rooms are common in Camas homes, while closets and crawlspaces add limits on size and venting.

  • Keep the heater close to the busiest fixtures to shorten the wait for hot water.
  • Leave room to service the unit and reach the shutoff valves.
  • Plan a drain or pan so a future leak does not reach the floor finish.
  • Confirm that a gas unit has proper venting and combustion air.
  • Check the electrical panel before choosing a heat pump or an electric tankless model.

If the new unit replaces a failed one, our replacement page explains the emergency path, and our plumbing installation page covers the other fixtures you might add during the same project.

Planning Around Permits and Utility Programs in Camas

The City of Camas Building Division administers the state-adopted Uniform Plumbing Code, and the 2021 I-Codes took effect on March 15, 2024. If your project changes venting, fuel or location, ask the Building Division whether a permit is required, and we will build that into the plan.

Clark Public Utilities has a heat pump water heater program for qualifying electric replacements, with eligibility based on the Northern Climate Specification. If you are building or remodeling, ask the utility what applies before you buy a unit.

Washington's version of the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) sets the standard for venting, relief valves and clearances, and the Camas Building Division enforces it, so a permit question is part of every install plan.

Safety Items We Check During Every Install

  • A temperature and pressure relief valve with a proper discharge line.
  • Seismic strapping where local conditions call for it.
  • A drain pan or drain path where a leak could cause damage.
  • Correct venting and combustion air for gas units.
  • A properly sized breaker and wire for electric units.

These are the details that keep a water heater safe for years. If something is out of date in your existing setup, we show you and explain why it matters.

Installation Questions

How long does a water heater installation take?

A like-for-like tank swap is often a same-day job, while a fuel change or relocation takes longer. We give you a realistic window when we quote it.

Can you install a heat pump water heater?

Yes, where the space and the electrical service allow it. Clark Public Utilities has a program for qualifying replacements, so check eligibility first.

Do I need a permit for a new water heater?

Often a permit applies, depending on the scope. We ask the Camas Building Division and tell you before work begins.

Do you install in Washougal as well?

Yes. We install in Washougal and the same code basics apply there with the local building department.

What if the old heater fails before the install date?

Call the emergency line. We can arrange a replacement through our repair and emergency service and tell you what is available.
