Plumbing on Prune Hill

Prune Hill is a residential part of Camas that sits above much of the city, with views that can reach the Columbia River and the Portland area. Local real estate guides describe established neighborhoods, custom homes, larger lots and mature landscaping, and Prune Hill Elementary is one of the Camas School District schools in the area.

Nobody publishes one official boundary for the name, so this page does not draw one. Live near Prune Hill Elementary, or in a subdivision your neighbors call part of Prune Hill? Then the plumbing notes below apply to you.

Why Lot Size and Slope Shape the Plumbing Conversation

The notes in this section are general plumbing information, not a claim about any particular Prune Hill street. A home on a larger lot usually has a longer run of pipe between the water meter and the house, and more outdoor fixtures to look after. A home on a slope has water and drain lines that can change height on the way in.

  • Long service lines. A slow leak between the meter and the house can run for weeks before it shows. Our water line repair page explains how failing service lines are found and replaced.
  • High water bills. If the bill climbs and no fixture is running, leak detection starts at the meter and works inward.
  • Outdoor spigots and irrigation. Hose bibs, sprinkler feeds and backflow assemblies are more common on bigger lots and are the first parts to freeze or fail.
  • Hot water. A tank that is leaking or no longer keeps up is a water heater replacement conversation about sizing and venting.

Know where the main shutoff is before you need it

Walk the house once and find the main valve, usually where the line enters the home or near the meter. Show everyone in the household. When a line fails, the minutes spent looking for a valve are the minutes water is running.

Irrigation, Backflow and Freezing

Properties with irrigation systems often have a backflow assembly, a device that keeps lawn water from flowing back into the drinking supply. The City of Camas expects these assemblies to be tested every year by a Certified Backflow Assembly Tester, so a sprinkler system adds a small yearly task to the list. Backflow protection also falls under the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as Washington adopts it.

Before the first hard freeze of the season, disconnect hoses, shut off and drain outdoor lines where the system allows, and open any low-point drain so trapped water has somewhere to go. It takes a few minutes and spares the fittings that sit outside the heated part of the house.

What the Camas Water Division Reports

Camas drinking water comes from groundwater wells and city reservoirs, run by the Public Works Water Division, which serves about 23,500 consumers. The division adds sodium hydroxide at treatment to help protect pipes from corrosion.

That matters for the copper and fittings inside your walls, and it is a good question to bring to any plumber who finds a pinhole leak: how old is the line, and what has the water chemistry been doing to it?

Plumbing Questions Prune Hill Homeowners Ask

Is Prune Hill an official neighborhood name?

It is a name residents, schools and local sources use for the area above much of Camas. Camas does not publish a single boundary for it, so ask us about your street by name instead of assuming you are inside or outside a line.

Can you help with a leak between the meter and my house?

Call and describe what you have noticed, such as a high bill, wet ground or low pressure. A water line repair or leak detection visit is where that kind of problem is sorted out.

Do I need to test my irrigation backflow device?

The City of Camas expects backflow assemblies to be tested annually by a Certified Backflow Assembly Tester. Check with the Water Division if you are not sure whether your assembly is on its list.

How do I know the plumber I hire is registered?

Plumbing work in Washington is regulated under RCW 18.106, and you can check any plumbing contractor on the Labor and Industries verification tool. Ask for the contractor name and registration number and look them up before work starts.
