Leaking or Cold? Do This Before the Plumber Arrives

If water is pooling around the base of the tank, turn the cold supply valve above the heater to the closed position and switch off power at the breaker or the gas control. Leave the hot taps closed until we arrive.

If the issue is only that the water is lukewarm or runs out quickly, you have more time. Note the age on the rating label, check whether the breaker has tripped, and look for a pilot light that went out.

No hot water

A tripped breaker, a failed element or thermostat, or a dead igniter are the usual suspects.

Water runs out fast

A broken dip tube or a sediment-filled tank leaves less usable hot water.

Popping or rumbling

Sediment baked onto the bottom of the tank rattles as it heats.

Rusty hot water

Corrosion inside the tank, or a worn anode rod, tints the hot side only.

What We Check When We Repair a Water Heater

A repair visit starts with safety. On a gas unit we check venting, gas connections and combustion air, and on an electric unit we check the breaker, the wiring and the elements before we replace anything.

  • Thermostats and elements on electric tanks that heat unevenly or not at all.
  • Igniters, thermocouples and gas valves on gas tanks that will not stay lit.
  • Temperature and pressure relief valves that drip, which can signal pressure or temperature trouble.
  • Anode rods and drain valves that wear out and shorten the life of the tank.
  • Tankless units with error codes, scale or flow sensor problems.

If the tank itself has failed, we tell you straight away so you do not pay for a repair that will not last. That conversation continues on our water heater replacement page.

Water Heaters, Camas Water and Local Rebates

The City of Camas treats its groundwater with chlorine and fluoride and adds sodium hydroxide to reduce copper pipe corrosion. The city's 2025 water quality report does not publish a hardness figure, so if you see scale on fixtures, a simple hardness test at home tells you whether sediment is likely to build up in your tank.

Clark Public Utilities runs a heat pump water heater program for Clark County homes that replace an electric resistance storage water heater, with eligibility tied to the Northern Climate Specification. Amounts and rules change, so check with the utility before you buy, and tell us if you plan to apply so we can match the unit to the program.

Relief valves, venting and clearances on water heaters are set by the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as Washington adopts it, so a repair visit looks at those standards and not only at the failed part.

Water Heater Repair Questions

Do you handle water heater emergencies at night or on weekends in Camas?

Yes. A leaking tank or a gas smell does not wait for business hours, and our line is open 24/7 at (360) 371-6092. If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility first.

How long should a water heater last?

Many tank units last around ten years, and the life depends on water quality and maintenance. The rating label shows the manufacture date, which helps decide between repair and replacement.

Why is my water heater making popping sounds?

Sediment has settled on the bottom of the tank and water trapped underneath is boiling through it. A flush may help on a younger tank, but heavy buildup is a sign of age.

Is a leaking water heater an emergency?

If the leak is from the tank body, yes. Shut off the cold supply and the power, and call. If it is from a fitting or relief valve, it is still worth a prompt visit.

Yes. We take repair calls in Washougal as well as Camas, and the same 24/7 line covers both.
