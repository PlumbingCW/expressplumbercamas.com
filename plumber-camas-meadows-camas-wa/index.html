Plumbing Priorities in Newer Communities

The points in this section are general. Newer homes are often built with current fixtures and a builder warranty, and the plumbing questions tend to be about upgrades and planning rather than age. Nothing here is a claim about the pipes in any specific Camas Meadows home.

  • Water heater sizing. A larger household or a new soaking tub can outgrow a standard tank. See tankless water heater for on-demand options and water heater installation for planned swaps.
  • Finishing and adding fixtures. A basement bath, wet bar or laundry move is plumbing installation work that needs a permit and an inspection.
  • Check the warranty first. If the home is under a builder warranty, read the terms before you pay for a repair. Some warranty coverage can be affected by outside work.
  • Everyday fixes. Faucets, toilets and disposals still wear out. See faucet repair.

Water, Code and Utility Questions

Plumbing work in Camas follows the 2021 Uniform Plumbing Code, which took effect on March 15, 2024, and permits and inspections go through the City of Camas Building Division. Ask the division before a water heater swap or a new bath so you know what paperwork applies.

Checking Who Does Plumbing Work at Your Home

Washington lets homeowners check any plumbing contractor on the Labor and Industries verification tool, which shows registration, bond and insurance status. Look up a contractor before work starts and keep the paperwork with your permit.

If the home is new enough to carry a builder warranty, keep copies of every plumbing receipt and permit. They show the date and scope of any work and can matter if a warranty question comes up later.

Plumbing Questions Camas Meadows Homeowners Ask

Is Camas Meadows one neighborhood?

The name covers a golf club and several communities. We use it for the area around Camas Meadows Golf Club and the Lake Road side streets, and we do not draw a single boundary. Give us your address when you call.

Should I check my builder warranty before calling a plumber?

Yes. Read what the warranty covers and whether it limits who may do the work. If a repair is covered, the builder may need to be the one to make it.

Do I need a permit to replace a water heater?

Many water heater replacements and new fixtures need a permit in Camas. Call the Building Division before the work starts so you know what applies.

What size water heater do I need for a bigger household?

It depends on how many people use hot water at the same time. Tankless water heater units are sized to the fixtures that must run at once, and a tank is sized to how much hot water the household uses in a busy hour.
