Sewer Backup: First Steps While You Wait

Stop running water in the house, including washing machines and dishwashers. Keep children and pets away from any backed-up area, and avoid touching standing wastewater with bare hands.

Open a window if there is odor, and do not use drain chemicals. Chemicals will not clear a break and make the line more hazardous for the next person who works on it.

How We Find What Failed in the Sewer Line

A camera on a flexible cable goes into the line through a clean-out and records what it sees. It shows roots, a sag that holds water, a crack, an offset joint or a collapse, and it tells us how far from the house the problem sits.

With that picture, the repair can be matched to the damage. A short section may be cut out and replaced, and a longer failure may call for a larger repair. You see the video and the plan before we start.

  • Spot repair replaces a short damaged section.
  • Partial replacement swaps a longer stretch of failed pipe.
  • Full replacement is considered when the whole line is failing.
  • Cleaning and root removal may come first so the camera can pass.

Who Owns What in the Camas Sewer System

In the City of Camas STEP system, the city owns the tank, the pump and the control panel, and the property owner maintains the line from the house to the tank. That ownership line tells us where our work stops, and we will say so before any digging.

Before any excavation, Washington law requires a call to 811 so utilities can mark buried lines, and utilities must mark at least two business days ahead under RCW 19.122. Plan for that lead time on any planned dig, and ask the Camas Building Division whether your repair needs a permit.

Sewer pipe material, slope and cleanout requirements come from the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as adopted in Washington through WAC 51-56, and a replacement section generally has to meet them.

Preparing Your Yard and Your Household

If we need to dig, we explain where the trench will go and what it will disturb. Clear the area of furniture and anything fragile, and let neighbors know if the work will affect shared property or driveways.

Plan for limited water use during the repair and ask us when service will be restored. After the work, we backfill and tell you how to care for the area while the soil settles.

Sewer Repair Questions

Do you handle sewer line emergencies at night or on weekends in Camas?

Yes. The line at (360) 371-6092 is open 24/7, and a sewer backup is exactly the call it is there for.

Can a sewer line be repaired without digging up the yard?

Sometimes. Camera findings tell us whether a spot repair or another method fits, and we explain the options before work starts.

How do I know if the problem is the sewer or just a drain?

If several fixtures back up together or a floor drain gurgles, the main line is the suspect. A cable and a camera confirm it.

What do I do about roots in my sewer line?

Roots are cut back first, and then the cause is addressed. A repair is the lasting fix if a joint or crack lets them in.
