When a restroom is out or a kitchen drain backs up, a business loses customers by the hour. Call (360) 371-6092 and we will talk through what is happening and how to keep the doors open while we fix it.

Business Plumbing Emergency? Protect the Space First

Shut off the water at the nearest valve, keep customers and staff away from wet floors, and move stock or paper records out of the way. If the problem is a sewage backup, close the affected area until it is cleaned.

Then call, and tell us if you rent or own the space. A tenant may need to notify the property manager, while an owner can approve work directly.

Commercial Work We Take On

  • Restaurant and food service drains, floor sinks, mop sinks and grease-related backups.
  • Restrooms with running toilets, slow urinals and leaking supply lines.
  • Water heaters sized for staff, customers and kitchen use.
  • Main line backups that affect a whole building.
  • Backflow prevention assembly service and testing coordination.
  • Tenant improvements with new fixtures and rough-in.

Repeat clogs in a kitchen line often point to heavy grease, and hydro jetting cleans the pipe wall instead of poking another hole. Water heater issues are handled on our repair page.

Backflow Testing and Camas Building Rules

The City of Camas requires backflow assemblies on underground irrigation and high-hazard connections to be tested annually by a Certified Backflow Assembly Tester. We help you identify whether your property has an assembly and coordinate the test so the paperwork is not forgotten.

Downtown Camas continues to change, with the Georgia-Pacific revitalization and demolition at the mill running from October 2025 through 2027 while papermaking continues. For tenant work, the Camas Building Division administers the state-adopted plumbing code, so ask it whether your project needs a permit.

Commercial fixtures, grease handling and backflow assemblies fall under the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as Washington adopts it, and the City of Camas backflow rules add annual testing for qualifying assemblies.

Keeping a Business Running During Repairs

A restaurant cannot serve without a working kitchen, and a shop cannot stay open without a restroom. We plan the work so the shutoff is as short as possible, isolate the affected line when we can and tell you plainly when a space must close for safety.

For planned jobs, we agree on hours that suit you. For emergencies, we act first and explain after, because a flooded floor or a backed-up kitchen cannot wait for a meeting.

Preventive Plumbing for Businesses

A scheduled visit to clean drains, check water heaters and test shutoffs can prevent the emergency that costs a day of business. We build a schedule that fits your traffic, so work happens in the slow hours.

Keep a plumbing record for the building, with shutoff locations, backflow test dates and past repairs, and give a copy to the property manager.
