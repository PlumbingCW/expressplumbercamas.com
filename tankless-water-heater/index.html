How a Tankless Water Heater Works in a Real Home

A tankless unit heats water as it flows through a heat exchanger, so there is no tank to run out. The limit is flow rate, which means one unit has a ceiling on how many showers, faucets and appliances it can feed at the same moment.

That ceiling is why sizing matters more than brand. A unit that is too small gives lukewarm water the moment a second bathroom starts, and a unit that is too large costs more for no benefit.

Sizing a Tankless Unit to Your Household

We count the fixtures that tend to run together, then add up the flow they need and the temperature rise between your incoming water and your set point. Incoming water in the Pacific Northwest is cold in winter, so the winter rise is the number that counts.

  • List every shower, tub, kitchen and laundry outlet in the home.
  • Note which ones commonly run at the same time, such as two morning showers.
  • Check the gas line size or the electrical panel capacity before choosing fuel.
  • Plan venting for gas models, which differs from a standard tank flue.
  • Decide whether a recirculation loop is worth adding for long pipe runs.

Water Chemistry and Scale in Camas

The City of Camas does not publish a hardness figure in its 2025 water quality report, so the safest approach is to test the water at your own tap or ask the water division. Hardness matters because scale builds on a tankless heat exchanger faster than most people expect.

If a test shows hard water, descaling goes on the calendar, and we can show you how a flush kit works. Manufacturers set their own flush schedule, so follow the label for your unit and tell us if you see an error code that points at flow or temperature.

Gas venting and gas piping for a tankless unit fall under the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) and the maker's installation standards, so the maker's instructions and the local code are followed together.

Tankless Descaling and Repair

Service for a tankless unit usually means a vinegar or descaling flush, a check of the inlet screen, and a look at venting and condensate lines. Error codes often trace back to scale or a restricted flow sensor.

Tankless Questions

Will a tankless heater work during a power outage?

Gas models still need electricity for the fan and controls, so most stop during an outage. Ask about a battery backup if outages are a concern in your area.

How often does a tankless heater need descaling?

It depends on the water and the manufacturer's schedule. Test your hardness first, then follow the schedule printed for your model.

Can I use tankless with a gas line I already have?

Sometimes, but many units draw more gas than a tank, so the line size and meter capacity must be checked.

Do you service tankless units in Washougal?

Yes, we service tankless heaters in Washougal as well as Camas.

Is tankless worth it for a small home?

It can be, especially if space is tight. We will say plainly when a standard tank or a heat pump tank is the better value.
