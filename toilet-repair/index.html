A toilet that will not stop running or will not flush at all can take over a household fast. Call (360) 371-6092 and we will walk you through the quick shutoff and plan the repair.

Overflowing Toilet? Stop the Water in 30 Seconds

Lift the tank lid and push the flapper down, or turn the small oval handle on the wall behind the toilet clockwise to close the supply valve. This stops more water entering the bowl.

Do not flush again. If water is already on the floor, towels along the base help, and if other drains are also slow, the problem is in the line rather than the toilet.

Toilet Problems We Fix

Running constantly

A worn flapper or a fill valve that never shuts off wastes water and raises the bill.

Weak or slow flush

Clogged rim jets, a low tank level or a partial clog in the trap.

Rocking base

A failed wax ring or loose closet bolts that need resetting.

Leak at the base

A broken seal, a cracked bowl or a loose supply connection.

Most toilet repairs are quick and use common parts. When the porcelain is cracked or the toilet is very old, replacement is the better value, and we can install a modern, water-saving model.

What Camas Sewer Rules Mean for Your Toilet

On the City of Camas STEP system, the city says flushable wipes are not suitable and that additives should not be used. A toilet that clogs repeatedly may be a sign of wipes or a partial blockage in the line you own, so we check both.

Because the city treats its water with sodium hydroxide to reduce copper pipe corrosion, the water is gentle on metal but still leaves mineral film in tanks over time. A small cleaning of the fill valve and flapper often restores a toilet that has started running on its own.

Toilet flush volume and installation clearances are covered by the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) and by federal water efficiency standards, which is why a replacement toilet should be a water-saving model.

A Few Quick Checks Before You Call

  • Is the toilet running because the flapper does not seal?
  • Is the water level in the tank too high, spilling into the overflow tube?
  • Is the chain tangled or too tight?
  • Is the bowl clogged only, or are other drains slow too?
  • Is the toilet rocking, which points to a seal or flange problem?

Telling us what you found helps us bring the right parts, and many toilets are repaired on the first visit.

Water Savings and Modern Toilets

Older toilets can use far more water per flush than newer models. A running toilet wastes even more, and a quiet leak can add up on the bill without anyone noticing. If your toilet is old and needs repeated repairs, replacement can pay for itself in saved water over time.

We can help you pick a replacement and install it properly, so the new toilet sits firmly and flushes well.

Why does my toilet run on its own?

A worn flapper or a stuck fill valve lets water leak from the tank to the bowl. The repair is quick and inexpensive.

Why does my toilet clog so often?

It can be a toilet design issue, wipes, or a partial blockage in the line. We check the toilet and the drain.

Can you replace a toilet?

Yes. We can remove the old toilet, set a new wax ring and install a modern model.
