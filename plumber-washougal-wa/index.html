Before We Arrive: Your Emergency Checklist

Shut off the water

Find the main shutoff, usually near the meter or where the line enters the house, and turn it clockwise until it stops.

Cut the power if water is near it

Switch off breakers to wet rooms if you can reach the panel without stepping in water.

Stop using drains

Do not flush or run the washer if a drain is backing up. More water has nowhere to go.

Clear a path

Move cars, boxes and pets so the plumber can reach the shutoff, the heater or the cleanout quickly.

Take photos

Photograph the damage before cleanup for your insurer.

Keep your phone on

If we need to ask a question or send directions, we will call back on the number you gave us.

Washougal Water, Wells and Old Buildings

The City of Washougal draws drinking water from wells, one field adjacent to Camas's Goot Park and another in the upper area of Hathaway Park, and it adds chlorine to the supply. The city's information does not state a hardness figure, so a simple home test is the way to learn whether scale is building up in your water heater and on your fixtures.

Washougal was incorporated in 1908 and the Pendleton Woolen Mill, which the Bishop family bought in 1912, shaped the town for decades. Older homes in the town center and near the mill tend to have older supply and drain lines, and that is where a camera look or a repipe discussion is most useful.

Highway 14 across Cape Horn opened in 1927 and the town has grown steadily to about 17,046 residents in the 2020 Census. That means newer subdivisions and older streets sit side by side, and each needs a different plumbing conversation.

Washougal falls under the same state-adopted Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as Camas, though the city has its own building office for permits, so we ask the local office what applies before any larger job.
