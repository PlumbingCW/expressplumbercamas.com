Our Home Base in Camas

Our address is 3504 NE 3rd Ave, Camas, WA 98607, and Camas ZIP 98607 is where most of our calls begin. The city runs from the old downtown near the paper mill to the Prune Hill neighborhoods on a long-extinct volcanic vent, and from Lacamas Lake and Heritage Park out toward Grass Valley.

Those parts of town have different plumbing. Downtown was rebuilt in brick after the 1923 fire, newer subdivisions on Prune Hill have modern lines, and some properties are connected to the city's STEP sewer system. We ask the right questions when you call so the right tools arrive.

How We Decide Whether an Address Is Covered

Three things set the answer. The first is distance from our shop on NE 3rd Ave, the second is what is wrong, and the third is what else is on the schedule that day. A burst pipe in Camas goes to the front of the line, while a planned fixture swap in a farther town can wait for a convenient day.

When you call, give us the street address and the nearest cross street. We tell you right away whether we can come, and if not, we say so plainly.

Local Details That Shape Our Work

  • STEP sewer connections. Many Camas properties connect to the city's system, where the city owns the tank, pump and panel and the owner maintains the line to the tank.
  • Water treatment. The city adds chlorine, fluoride and sodium hydroxide, and it does not report hardness, so we recommend a home test when scale is a concern.
  • Winter freezes. The January 2024 storm burst pipes at local schools, so we plan for freeze protection in every season.
  • Digging rules. Washington requires a call to 811 before any excavation, with utilities marking at least two business days ahead.
  • Permits. The Camas Building Division enforces the state-adopted plumbing code, and Washougal has its own building department.

Washougal has its own wells, with one well field adjacent to Camas's Goot Park and another in the upper area of Hathaway Park. The two cities share a border and plenty of plumbing questions, which is why our Washougal page has its own checklist.

What to Do Before You Call

If water is running, shut off the main valve first, and do not use any drain that is backing up. If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 and your gas utility before you call us. Keep your phone handy so we can call back with questions.

Then tell us your street address, what you see, hear or smell, and what you have already tried. A few honest answers help us bring the right tools, and our burst pipe repair and drain cleaning pages explain what to expect.

Seasonal Calls Across the Area

Winter brings frozen hose bibs, split supply lines and failed water heaters. Spring brings slow drains after the wet season and yard leaks as the ground shifts. Summer is irrigation and backflow season, and fall is the time to insulate, drain outdoor lines and check the shutoff.

Knowing the season helps us bring the right parts to the door. It also helps you plan, which is why our guides on freeze protection, water heaters and drains are written with Camas conditions in mind.
