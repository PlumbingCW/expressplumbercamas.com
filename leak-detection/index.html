Suspect a Leak? Do the Meter Test Before You Call

Shut off every fixture and appliance, then read your water meter. Wait fifteen minutes without using any water and read it again. If the numbers changed, water is moving through the house or the yard line.

If your meter is digital, watch the leak indicator. A blinking faucet icon means water has flowed at least half the day, every day, and a steady icon means flow all day. That matches the guidance the City of Camas gives its customers.

How We Find a Leak Without Opening Walls

We narrow the search in steps. First we isolate the house from the street to learn whether the leak is before or after the main shutoff, then we listen and trace along the suspected run.

  • Pressure testing isolates one supply line at a time.
  • Acoustic listening picks up the sound of water escaping under pressure.
  • Thermal imaging shows warm or cold paths behind finishes when a hot line leaks.
  • Meter and valve checks confirm whether the leak is in the house, the yard or a fixture.
  • Camera inspection looks inside a drain for leaks into the soil.

When the leak is found, the repair path depends on the pipe. Water supply line leaks flow into water line repair, and failed pipe in the walls may be a case for repiping.

Leaks, Meters and Billing Adjustments in Camas

The City of Camas explains that a leak adjustment on a water bill applies when consumption is more than twice the average for the period under Camas Municipal Code 13.44.030. The sewer charge credit applies only to leaks in the months of November through February. Those rules come from the city's own pages, so check the current wording with the city before you plan around a credit.

The city also asks customers to wait 24 hours after a repair before judging the meter. That is a good reason to keep the repair receipt and the date of the work, because a plumber's findings are the simplest way to show the city that a leak was real and has been fixed.

The Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), as Washington adopts it, sets test requirements for new and altered plumbing, so a leak repair that opens a wall may have to meet them before it is closed up.

How can I tell if my high water bill is a leak?

Run the meter test and check the leak indicator. If the meter moves with everything off, there is a leak, and finding it is the next step.

Do you tear into walls to find a leak?

We trace first with listening, pressure and imaging tools so any opening is small and in the right place.

Often yes, using pressure testing and acoustic tools. The repair can then be planned around the exact location.
