Family Homes and the Jobs That Come Up Most

The notes in this section are general. Busy family homes tend to wear out the same handful of parts: drains in kitchens and baths, toilets that run or clog, faucets that drip, disposals that jam and water heaters that age out. None of that points to a problem on any Grass Valley street, but it is a useful checklist.

  • Slow kitchen and bath drains. Grease and hair are the usual cause. See drain cleaning.
  • Toilets that run, rock or clog. Often a small part and a quick fix. See toilet repair.
  • Disposals that jam or hum. See garbage disposal repair.
  • Hot water. A leaking or noisy tank leads to water heater repair or a replacement plan.

Wipes belong in the trash

Wipes sold as flushable do not break down like toilet paper. The City of Camas also says they are not suitable for its STEP sewer systems, so keep them out of the toilet whichever kind of sewer connection you have.

Sewer Connections and Who Owns What

Some Camas properties connect to the city's STEP system, a small pump tank between the house and the street. The city owns the tank, pump and control panel, and the owner maintains the line from the house to the tank. We do not know which connection a given Grass Valley address has, so ask the City of Camas before you assume.

In practical terms, a clog in the line you own is something to clear, and a sounding alarm panel at the tank is a matter for the city. Knowing which side of that line your problem sits on saves a wasted visit.

Plumbing Questions Grass Valley Homeowners Ask

What counts as Grass Valley in Camas?

The name is used for a residential area on the west side of Camas around Grass Valley Elementary and the Grass Valley street names. There is no single published boundary, so give us your address when you call.

My sink drains slowly. Do I need a plumber?

One slow sink is often a clog close to the fixture and may clear with a simple tool. If several drains slow down together, the blockage is probably in the main line and worth a proper look. See drain cleaning.

How do I know if my water heater is failing?

Warning signs include rust-colored hot water, rumbling noises, a leak at the base and water that never gets hot. A leaking tank is a replacement, not a repair.

Who maintains the sewer line outside my house?

It depends on your connection. On the STEP system the owner maintains the line from the house to the tank and the city owns the tank. Ask the City of Camas which applies to your address.
