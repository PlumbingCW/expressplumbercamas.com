Main Line Clogged? Do This Right Now

Stop using water, including laundry and dishwasher cycles. The lowest drain in the house, usually a basement floor drain or a tub on the ground floor, will show the backup first.

Do not flush again to see if it clears. Call, and we will ask a few questions about where the water appears and whether the outside alarm panel is sounding if your home is on a STEP connection.

How Main Line Cleaning Works

We locate the clean-out nearest the problem and run a cable machine with a cutting head through the line. The cutter opens a channel through roots or packed debris, and we pull the material back as it comes loose.

Afterward we run water and put a camera down the line. If it is clean and sound, we leave it clear. If it is damaged, we show you the video and explain the repair options, which you can compare on our sewer line repair page.

  • Cutter heads for roots and hard buildup.
  • Retriever heads to pull out wipes and packed paper.
  • Water flushing to carry the remains down the line.
  • Camera check to confirm the pipe is open and see its condition.

STEP Sewer Systems and What Not to Flush in Camas

The City of Camas runs a STEP sewer system in parts of town, and it asks owners to keep the green tank lids uncovered with at least 1 inch of clearance and to leave the outside alarm panel unobstructed. The city says additives should not be used and that flushable wipes are not suitable, and it pumps the tank every six to ten years.

If your alarm is sounding, that is a tank issue on the city's side. We can clear the line you own and tell you whether the line looks clear to the tank, then the city handles its own equipment.

The Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) requires cleanouts at certain points on a building sewer so the line can be cleared, which is why a missing or buried cleanout is worth fixing during a cleaning visit.

How to Keep a Sewer Line From Backing Up Again

Keep wipes, paper towels, grease and hygiene products out of the toilet and sink. Watch large trees near the line, since roots follow moisture and enter at joints. If you have had a backup before, a camera look and a plan for periodic cleaning can save you the next one.

A line that clogs more than once a year deserves a closer look, not a third cleaning. A repair or replacement may cost more now and far less over the years.

What You Can Expect During the Visit

We start with a conversation about what you saw and when it began. Then we find the access point, set up the machine, clear the line and run a camera to check the result. We explain what we found and what, if anything, comes next.

You will see us put down protection at the entry point and clean up afterward. If the line serves a rental or a business, we can send the findings to the property manager on request.

Sewer Cleaning Questions

Do you handle sewer cleaning emergencies at night or on weekends in Camas?

Yes. Call (360) 371-6092 any hour, and we will tell you what we can do that night.

How do I know the clog is in the sewer and not the house drain?

If more than one fixture backs up or the lowest drain gurgles, the sewer is likely. A camera confirms it.

Will cleaning damage my sewer line?

Done correctly on sound pipe, no. We camera the line to see its condition, and if it is fragile we say so.

What should I never flush?

Wipes, even ones labeled flushable, along with grease, paper towels and feminine products. They collect in the line and in a STEP tank.
